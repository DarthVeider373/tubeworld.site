About Precious Hair and Beauty Salon Limited

PRECIOUS HAIR AND BEAUTY SALON LIMITED is a private limited company registered in England and Wales. The salon is connected to the Croydon community and focuses on practical, dependable hair and beauty services for local clients.

From a routine refresh to a complete style update, the team aims to make the process calm, clear and comfortable from the first enquiry through to the finished look.
